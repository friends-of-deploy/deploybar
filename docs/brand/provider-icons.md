# Provider icons

The GitHub, Azure DevOps, and Vercel SVGs are from
[Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0),
distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md).
Brand trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Sources:

- [GitHub](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/11.15.0/icons/github.svg)
- [Azure DevOps](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/11.15.0/icons/azuredevops.svg)
- [Vercel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/11.15.0/icons/vercel.svg)

Assets live in `DeployBar/Assets.xcassets/Provider*.imageset`. Each keeps the
original 24 × 24 viewBox and paths, with an explicit 14 × 14 pt intrinsic size
for native menus. All three use template rendering and preserve vector data,
so macOS supplies the appropriate tint for light, dark, selected, and disabled
states. Account rows use the same 14 pt size; onboarding scales them to 20 pt.
